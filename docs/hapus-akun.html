Hapus akun & semua data

Monthly Budget Planner (com.yuda.budgetplanner)

English

Dari aplikasi (disarankan)

  1. Buka aplikasi, ketuk ikon profil di kanan atas.
  2. Gulir ke bawah, ketuk Hapus akun & semua data.
  3. Baca konfirmasinya, lalu ketuk Hapus permanen.

Tombol ini hanya muncul bila Anda masuk dengan Google. Bila tidak pernah masuk, tidak ada akun maupun data di Drive; semua data hanya di HP dan terhapus saat aplikasi di-uninstall (atau lewat Anggaran › Data & Cadangan › Kosongkan Buku Kas).

Penghapusan berlaku seketika. Bila HP tidak terhubung ke internet sehingga file di Drive gagal dihapus, data di HP tidak diubah dan aplikasi meminta Anda mencoba lagi, agar tidak tersisa data di Drive tanpa sepengetahuan Anda.

Pengembang tidak punya server dan tidak menyimpan salinan data Anda, jadi tidak ada data yang tersisa pada pengembang.

Bila aplikasi sudah di-uninstall

  1. Data di HP sudah ikut terhapus saat uninstall.
  2. Hapus file aplikasi di Google Drive: buka drive.google.com, cari file bernama BudgetPlanner_Sinkron_Otomatis.json dan BudgetPlanner_Backup_….json, hapus, lalu kosongkan Sampah Drive.
  3. Cabut akses aplikasi: myaccount.google.com/permissions › Monthly Budget Planner › Hapus akses.

Butuh bantuan? Buka Issues di GitHub.

Delete your account & data (English)

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open the app and tap the profile icon (top right).
  2. Scroll down and tap Hapus akun & semua data (Delete account & all data).
  3. Confirm with Hapus permanen (Delete permanently).

This permanently deletes the app's sync and backup files in your Google Drive, including old backups already in Drive's trash, revokes the app's access to your Google account, and deletes all data on the phone: cash books, categories, budgets, transactions, recurring transactions, profile, and local backup copies. Device settings without financial data (e.g. app lock) remain until uninstall. The button appears only when signed in; without sign-in there is no account or Drive data, and uninstalling removes everything. Deletion is immediate. The developer has no server and retains no copy of your data.

If the app is already uninstalled

On-device data was removed on uninstall. Delete the files BudgetPlanner_Sinkron_Otomatis.json and BudgetPlanner_Backup_….json at drive.google.com (then empty the trash) and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Help: GitHub Issues.
